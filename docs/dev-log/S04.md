# S04 — Domain contracts, verdict disposition, rule pack

**Goal:** fix the vocabulary every later number depends on: what the LLM may say (`VerifierOutput`),
what the system concludes (`Verdict`, `Disposition`), how a claim with several citations is judged, and
what a rule pack is. These definitions are pre-registered in S15, so they must be total and fail closed.

## Built

- `features/contracts/`:
  - `ids.py`: `ClaimId`, `SourceId`.
  - `labels.py`: `Label` (`supported`, `overstated`, `contradicted`, `not_in_source`), `EvidenceStatus`,
    `NegationCheck`, `OverstatedReason` (9 reasons + `other`), `Disposition`, `FailureKind`.
  - `verdict.py`: `Claim`, `VerifierOutput`, `Verdict` (`finalise`, `verify_against`), `VerifierFailure`,
    `disposition()` (the total 4×4×3 table), `claim_disposition()` (worst case over exactly the cited
    pairs), `verifier_output_json_schema()`.
  - `rulepack.py`: `RuleExample`, `Rule`, `RulePack` (frozen; every reason except `other` has a rule).
- `infrastructure/rulepack_yaml.py`: a SafeLoader that refuses duplicate keys, anchors, aliases, merge
  keys, non-plain scalars and very deep nesting; errors carry the file, line or field path.
- `data/rulepacks/default.yaml`: the v0 pack, 9 rules, fictional entities, all author-written
  (`reviewed_by: []`). Ships in the wheel (tested).
- CLI: `bio-claim-check rulepack validate [PATH]` (exit 2 with one line per problem).
- `schemas/verifier_output.schema.json`, `schemas/rulepack.schema.json`, generated by `make schemas`
  and checked for freshness; the LLM-facing one is walked for strict-mode compatibility.
- Construction guard: outside `contracts/verdict.py`, a `Verdict` is made only by `finalise` or
  `model_validate`.
- `Span` rejects non-`int` offsets (`True`, `1.0`, `"1"`).
- `make cov-common` → `make cov-core` (common + features.contracts, 100% branch).
- `pyyaml` is a runtime dependency, classified app-only (`yaml` is in `core-has-no-driver-imports`).
- ADR 0006, `docs/glossary.md` with an enum parity test, invariants I16/I17, I12 gains `yaml`, backlog
  A5 ✅ and A5b ✅.

## Expert consult and plan review

The expert consult (unit of analysis, label set, rule-pack provenance) and the 19-finding plan review
are triaged in the session plan; the decisions are summarised in ADR 0006. Main outcomes:
- The (claim, source) pair is the unit; the claim takes the worst of its pairs.
- `unsupported` became `contradicted`; `VERIFIED_SUPPORTED` became `passed`.
- `FAILED` keeps pipeline failures in the denominators.
- Negation is tri-state; `not_run` can never be `passed`, so until S10 nothing passes. That is deliberate.
- Cut: reproducibility fields on the Verdict, a `flag_basis` field, a root union schema, YAML line
  mapping for model errors, installing the wheel.

## Review triage

### Implementation review
One paranoid review of the diff after `make verify` was green. Eight findings, all adopted:

| # | Severity | Finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | A claim forged with duplicate cites (`model_copy(update=…)`) shrinks the pairs a claim is judged on | **Adopt:** `model_copy(update=…)` raises on `Claim`, `Verdict` and `VerifierFailure`; `claim_disposition` re-checks that cites are non-empty and unique |
| 2 | High | A replayed verdict could set `negation: clear` and turn `needs_review` into `passed`; `verify_against` accepted it | **Adopt:** `finalise` takes a negation *detector*, not a value; `verify_against` re-runs it and refuses a stored `clear`/`negated` without one |
| 3 | Medium | The guard missed `V = Verdict`, `getattr(…, "model_construct")` and `BaseModel.model_copy(…)` | **Adopt, as a backstop only:** the three patterns are flagged; `verify_against` stays the integrity check |
| 4 | Medium | Deeply nested YAML raised `RecursionError` (CLI exit 1, not 2) | **Adopt:** reported as a rule-pack error, "nesting is too deep" |
| 5 | Low | The strict-schema walk skipped a schema-valued `additionalProperties` / `items` | **Adopt** |
| 6 | Low | `reviewed_by` allowed the same reviewer twice | **Adopt** |
| 7 | Low | `Claim.cites` accepted a set, so citation order was lost | **Adopt:** only a list or tuple |
| 8 | Low | The default pack named a real drug | **Adopt:** replaced by "toxin-induced" |

Fixes were verified by `make verify` and targeted tests, not re-reviewed (one review per session).

### Deviations from the plan
- `Verdict.finalise(..., negation_detector=None)` replaces the planned `negation: NegationCheck`
  parameter (finding #2).
- `Claim`, `Verdict` and `VerifierFailure` share a `_Validated` base that refuses unvalidated copies.
- The invariants are I16/I17 as planned.
