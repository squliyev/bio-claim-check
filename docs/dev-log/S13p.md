# S13p — The proof checkpoint on dev-v1

**Goal:** a first exploratory reading on the demo track. Does the frozen verifier (freeze v2) flag the
planted traps of dev-v1 without flagging their parents? The go/adjust rule and the pair tables were
committed before any trap was verified (ADR 0024). dev-v1 is a **silver standard**, confirmed by a model
panel (ADR 0023): 38 items, of which 16 are parents, 15 model traps and 7 rule-floor traps.

## Built

| Commit | What |
|---|---|
| A (this commit) | Pair tables in `TrapCounts` (`features/traps/counts.py`), recomputed by CI under I76. `app/usecases/proof_checkpoint.py`: the ADR 0024 rule, bound to dev-v1's sha256 and denominators. `traps verify` prints the pair tables and the verdict, and refuses a second `--record` before any call. ADR 0024, I80, glossary |

**Deviations from the plan, with reasons:**
- **The rule is in `app/usecases`, not the traps slice.** It is a demo decision about one set, not a
  generic measure (review #6).
- **Parents passed ≥ 12 of 16 was added** to the maintainer's default (≥ 10 of 15 model traps flagged,
  ≤ 3 of 16 parents flagged, ≤ 2 of 38 failed). It stops a run that sends most parents to `needs_review`
  from passing. It was added before any call (review #1).
- **The pair table is the full 4 by 4 table**, not two cells (review #7).

## Plan review (paranoid senior engineer, 8 findings)

| # | Finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | GO could pass while most parents go to `needs_review` | **Adopt:** parents passed ≥ 12 of 16 added before any call |
| 2 | Hiding the counts under ADJUST is predeclared publication bias, against ADR 0023 §5 | **Adopt:** the counts are shown either way; the verdict changes only the framing |
| 3 | The verdict is not attested by CI | **Adapt:** an arch test recomputes the verdict from the committed result and checks the line stated here (I80). Policy id `adr-0024-v1`. The result format is unchanged, because the rule is not a property of a generic result |
| 4 | The rerun wording does not match the code: transport errors abort the run; counted failures replay from the cache | **Adopt:** ADR 0024 §4 states the real semantics; every invocation is logged here |
| 5 | A second `--record` runs the whole job before it is refused; attempt files are not unique | **Adopt** the preflight (refused before any call, tested). **Reject** unique attempt ids: `src` has no clock or randomness, and attempts are content-addressed by design. Invocations are listed here, self-reported |
| 6 | The thresholds encode dev-v1's sizes and belong in app policy | **Adopt:** `app/usecases/proof_checkpoint.py`, bound to the set's sha256 and exact denominators; it fails closed |
| 7 | Two cells hide the paired behaviour; shared parents are not independent | **Adopt:** the full trap × parent disposition table; the non-independence is stated in `counts.py` and ADR 0024 |
| 8 | "At most 76 calls" is not a ceiling on provider attempts | **Adopt:** "76 logical calls, plus bounded transport retries"; the usage tally is reported below |
