answer from one that Europe PMC returned. Either one breaks the closed test split (ADR 0020, I74).

## The six checks (2026-10-26; 6 live verifier calls, as announced)

Purpose-built demo cases: **not sampled, not an estimate, not expert-labelled.** None of this is shown on
the day (option C).

| Paper | Role | Intended kind | Disposition | Label | Run id |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MED:37729419` | faithful | n/a | `passed` | supported | `96410a369775d632` |
| `MED:37729419` | overstated | `preclinical_to_clinical` | `flagged` | overstated (`preclinical_to_clinical`) | `63ba31f6d0bd1fe9` |
| `MED:37852737` | faithful | n/a | `passed` | supported | `ba2ad6e62cb518af` |
| `MED:37852737` | overstated | `background_as_result` | `flagged` | **contradicted** | `274e8153d8b00198` |
| `MED:34815662` | faithful | n/a | **`needs_review`** | supported | `9e05490ef8204493` |
| `MED:34815662` | overstated | `nonsignificant_as_effect` | `flagged` | **contradicted** | `91fe6c6172496987` |

What this shows, as recorded:
- **Each overstated claim was flagged.** Two were flagged as `contradicted`, not as the intended overstated
  kind. The verifier's reasons say why: the trial found no efficacy, and the abstract states that there was
  no significant change.
- **The null-result faithful claim went to `needs_review`, not `passed`.** That is the deterministic
  negation rule: `supported` with a negated located quote goes to `needs_review` (`contracts/verdict.py`).
  It is the same pattern S13p reported for parents. This is recorded, not tuned.
- **Nothing is swapped and nothing is tuned.**

For a pitch, only the preclinical case gives the intended label on both claims. Under option C the slide
is hand-made in any case.
